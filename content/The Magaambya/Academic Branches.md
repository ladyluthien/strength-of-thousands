Magaambya scholars believe in many fundamental values of scholarship. Key among them are adaptability, camaraderie, imagination, knowledge, and valor. The school’s faculty and lessons are divided into five branches, each emphasizing one of these values.

- **Cascade Bearers** esteem imagination, from the dreams of the most visionary magical theorists to the innovations of creative problem-solvers of all stripes. 
	- Common skill checks for this branch include Arcana and Engineering. 
	- Faculty in this branch include Takulu Ot and Lesedi. 
	- Students in this branch include Chizire and Mariama Keitana.

- **Emerald Boughs** emphasize camaraderie, the sense of community and connection to fellow Magaambyans; they also interact with those outside the Magaambya and spread the academy’s message. 
	- Common skill checks for this branch include Culture and Religion. 
	- Faculty in this branch include Zuma and Janatimo. 
	- Students in this branch include Ignaci Canterells and Strands-of-Glowing-Dawn Tzeniwe.

- **Rain-Scribes** value adaptability in thoughts and deeds, as well as a willingness to admit errors and learn from past mistakes. 
	- Common skill checks for this branch include Medicine and Survival. 
	- Faculty in this branch include Izem Mezitani and Koride Uwala. 
	- Students in this branch include Anchor Root and Haibram Thodja.

- **Tempest-Sun Mages** inspire valor, not only the physical courage to stand up for friends and the school, but also the intellectual courage to consider opposing viewpoints and widen one’s understanding rather than staying entrenched in a single paradigm.
	- Common skill checks for this branch include Performance and Persuasion. 
	- Faculty in this branch include Mafika Ayuwari and Tahenkot. 
	- Students in this branch include Esi Djana.

- **Uzunjati** understand that knowledge lives in stories passed down through the generations and also is earned every day through personal experience. 
	- Common skill checks for this branch include History and Insight. 
	- Faculty in this branch include Ahassunu and Nhyira. 
	- Students in this branch include Noxolo and Okoro Obiyo. 

Each student joins one primary branch to shape their academic understanding, but all Magaambyans are expected to embody these values to varying degrees, and members of the different branches work together on and off campus. You should choose a branch that’s the primary focus of your character. Most of your class study is focused on advancing your knowledge in this primary branch. You should also choose a secondary branch that supplements your education, as you’ll advance in this secondary branch as well. It’s fine—in fact, it’s preferred—if the characters have a variety of branches.
