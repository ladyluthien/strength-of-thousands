This is a hack of the Pathfinder academic progression system with the A5e Prestige mechanic. When you would gain Prestige for any reason short of Strongholds, you gain Academic Prestige instead. Your Academic Prestige is specific to your [[Academic Branches]] and can never be higher than your level for your main branch, or half your level for your secondary branch.

Simply studying is not enough to gain Academic Prestige - the Magaambya is not a "Cs get degrees" kind of school. Rather, it is earned through being truly exceptional; developing new techniques, brokering new connections, or recovering rare magical artifacts. However, a critical success on an exam increases your Academic Prestige by 1.

#### Academic Prestige
1. You gain a new skill specialty in one of your branch skills. 
2. You gain an expertise die on checks that relate to your branch.
3. You can ask a favor of another member of your branch, so long as the favor does not take longer than half an hour to complete and would not have an adverse effect on the person you ask the favor of.
4. You can ask a favor of a non-Magaambyan that would look favorably on your branch, so long as the favor does not take longer than half an hour to complete and would not have an adverse effect on the person you ask the favor of.
5. You gain an expertise die on Deception, Persuasion, and Intimidation checks against members of your branch.
6. Starting at this level, you cannot roll lower than your Academic Prestige on a skill check that relates to your branch.
7. You gain access to specialized training through your branch. Your expertise die on skill specialities in your branch skills increases to a 1d6.
8. Your knowledge of the specific has improved your knowledge of the general. Once per rest, you can add an expertise die to a skill you are not proficient in.
9. You gain a new skill specialty in one of your branch skills.
10. Members of your branch admire you. You can ask favors that take several hours or that would otherwise inconvenience the person you ask the favor of.
11. Non-Magaambyans that look favorably on your branch have heard of your accomplishments. You can ask favors that take several hours or that would otherwise inconvenience the person you ask the favor of.
12. Your expertise die on Deception, Persuasion, and Intimidation checks against members of your branch increases to a 1d6.
13. Your expertise die on skill specialties in your branch skills increases to a 1d6.
14. You gain a cantrip of your choice from the following:
	1. **Cascade Bearers:** *prestidigitation, thaumaturgy*
	2. **Emerald Boughs:** *friends, guidance*
	3. **Rain-Scribes**: *light, pestilence*
	4. **Tempest Sun-Mages:** *resistance, shillelagh*
	5. **Uzunjati:** *circular breathing, calculate*
15. You gain a new skill specialty in one of your branch skills.
16. You cannot roll lower than a 10 on a roll that uses a skill specialty from one of your branch schools, picked when you reach this level.
17. Members of your branch see you as a paragon of their order. You may ask a member of your branch to make a significant sacrifice for you - but if the sacrifice is too great or the result is unworthy, you may lose a level or more of Academic Prestige with this branch.
18. You gain a first-level spell of your choice from the following, which you can cast once per rest: 
	1. **Cascade Bearers:** *detect magic, silent image*
	2. **Emerald Boughs:** *comprehend languages, traveler's ward*
	3. **Rain-Scribes**: *longstrider, speak with animals*
	4. **Tempest Sun-Mages:** *fortified will, harmonic residence*
	5. **Uzunjati:** *ceremony, identify*
19. You cannot roll lower than a 10 on a roll that uses a skill specialty from one of your branch schools, picked when you reach this level.
20. You have reached the top of your branch and receive the following capstone benefit:
	1. **Cascade Bearers:** Once per day, you can cast *programmed illusion* or *project image* (not both).
	2. **Emerald Boughs:** Once per week, you can summon up a *magnificent mansion*.
	3. **Rain-Scribes**: You ignore the effects of severe cold and heat, and you are permanently under the effects of *freedom of movement*.
	4. **Tempest Sun-Mages:** Once per day, you can cast *shield*, affecting anyone of your choice within five feet of you as well as yourself.
	5. **Uzunjati:** Once per day, you can cast *legend lore* without consuming any material components.
