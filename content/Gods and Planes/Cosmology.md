Regardless of how they are seen by the peoples of the Material Plane, every god has divine essence and has since the beginning of creation. This divine essence can be shared, and the easiest way to touch divine [[Magic]] is to pledge oneself to a god. This divine essence flows through the planes of Good and Evil, Law and Chaos, like veins through a piece of marble. Rarely, it extends to other planes, although never past [[the River]] unless it is in a material conduit - usually, a person. 

On the other side of the cosmic scale are the [[Oppodei]]. Where they came from is something known only to whatever preceded creation; however, as long as gods have existed, so have they. While all fiends are minor Oppodei, only the most powerful Oppodei possess the definitive power - the ability to corrupt divine essence. For this reason, gods rarely combat the Oppodei directly. Instead, they send mortals to fight their holy wars. Oppodei, for their part, cannot grant spells, but can serve as warlock patrons. 

Aberrations are not Oppodei, but they use psionics with enough power that they can warp both divine essence and material space alike. Very, very rarely, aberrations with human-like intelligence and the ability for psionics (aboleths, mind flayers, khalkoi, and the like) can develop humanoid traits and ethics. Some scholars even claim that such creatures have souls that resemble human souls. However, most aberrations never pursue such a path; for a mind flayer or khalkos, the transformation is as uncanny as a humanoid turning into a mind flayer would be to a human, and aboleths see such a transition as beneath them.
