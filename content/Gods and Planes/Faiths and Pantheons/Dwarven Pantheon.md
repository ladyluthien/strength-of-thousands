Dwarves acknowledge Moradin as their creator and believe that the first dwarves were not born, but rather smithed by him. They also honor Moradin's court, the Morndinsamman. The members of the Morndinsamman are:

- Berronar, the Revered Mother and Moradin's wife. If Moradin crafted the dwarves, she was the one to give them rules to live by. 
- Haela, the Lady of the Fray. She blesses the dwarves in joyful battle.
- Dugmaren, the Tinker. He blesses the dwarves when they craft and seek new knowledge.
- Vergadain, the Merchant. He blesses all commerce.
- Marthammor, the Finder of Trails. He blesses travel and curiosity and is somewhat seen as a wayward son.
- Dramora, the Plantmistress. She blesses all things that grow, rather than are forged; mushrooms, moss, and even living fibers like sheep. If Marthammor is a wayward son, she is a wayward daughter.
