Goblinoids share a common ancestry and a common spirituality; however, of all the religious traditions, theirs may well have the most conflict. 

In the goblinoid tradition, there were initially many gods, all of them battling for dominion with each other. In this great conflict, one eventually rose above all the rest: Maglubiyet, the Conquerer. He slew the other gods and became a stern ruler. Goblins, hobgoblins, and bugbears were compelled to conduct sacrifices to earn his intervention - the grander the sacrifice, the more powerful the intervention. 

Two hundred years ago, a group of goblinoid adventurers grew in strength on the Material Plane. After many grand deeds, it is said that they were able to transverse the Planes themselves and find Maglubiyet's domain. There, it is said they killed him. On the Material, all that is known for certain is that clerics to Maglubiyet suddenly and irreversibly lost their power.

Goblinkind fractured. Many celebrated that they would no longer have to sacrifice and became functionally atheist. Others began to venerate the adventurers as new gods, the Five. Still others claimed that Maglubiyet could not be killed. This group has two distinct factions, those that believe that Maglubiyet has turned his back on goblinkind for their heresy and must be brought back with even more and bloodier sacrifices, and those who believe that in dying and being reborn, Maglubiyet was humbled and remade into a kinder deity that would grant intervention with prayer alone, rather than sacrifice. Finally, there are those who believe that their people killed their god and that no redemption is possible for the world. These goblinoids can be very, *very* dangerous.
