All peoples, regardless of faith, know the world is trapped in the balance of Good and Evil, Law and Chaos. They also acknowledge the existence of other planes of existence, layered around the Material in a way that scholars continue to debate. What is certain is that there is a cosmic **[[the River|River]]** of sorts that separates the lands of the living from the immortal lands. No immortal creature can cross physically into the lands of the living, and no living creature can pass into the immortal realms without dying. 

The lands of the living comprise the Material Plane; the Fey; the Fey's shadowy cousin, the Bleak Gate; and the four Elemental Planes. The Fey and the Bleak Gate are considered inverses of each other, where the Fey tends more towards Chaos and the Bleak Gate towards Law. However, entities of the Fey and the Bleak Gate still possess free will, as far as people have been able to tell. Likewise, each element has its inverse; Fire and Water, Earth and Air. Scholars have argued that beyond [[the River]] may lie corresponding planes of Law, Chaos, Good, and Evil, but these planes, if they exist, rarely exert their pressure on any but the most noteworthy individuals. 

What most faiths believe, however, is in the existence of the Upper and Lower planes. Upper planes are strongly associated with Good, but can be Lawful or Chaotic; Lower planes are likewise unbound but strongly associated with Evil. Scholars have attempted to map these planes, but so few people return from the dead that such maps are more speculation than anything. However, those that do return speak of crossing a grey river in the arms of a comforting, grey-robed figure and emerging into a realm that they believe is the afterlife. This has lead to the commonly shared belief in [[the River]].

In between the Material, Dreaming, and Bleak Gate lies the Ethereal, a misty mirror of the world of the living. Around the Elemental Plane lies the Astral, a grey wasteland that eventually borders the River.
