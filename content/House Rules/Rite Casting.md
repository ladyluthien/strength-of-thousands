Sometimes, spellcasters need to work together to create more powerful, longer-lasting effects. These rites function somewhere between a spell and an exploration challenge; the primary caster must make an ability check, but secondary casters can assist to give a bonus. Depending on the degree of success or failure on the ability check, the rite may have different effects.

Rites have levels like spells do. You cannot cast a rite with a level higher than the highest-level spell that you can cast. Rites cannot be earned on levelling up and must be researched or acquired through adventuring. Rites often take hours or even days to cast and may have material components.

Like exploration challenges, however, a rite has a primary check and often a handful of secondary checks. The primary check is always made with the caster's spellcasting ability and is typically a very high DC. Secondary checks *may* be made with other abilities, depending on the rite, and are a lower DC. Each successful secondary check adds +1 to the final DC; a critical success adds +3, while a failure adds a -1 penalty and a critical failure adds a -3 penalty. Succeeding on the primary check by 5 or more triggers a critical success on the rite, while failing by 5 or more triggers a critical failure.

Sometimes, there may be situational bonuses that can add an expertise die to the overall rite; for example, a rite developed by garoul may be easier under a full moon, or a fey rite inside a ring of toadstools. Likewise, sometimes there may be situational penalties, such as substituting a component, that grant disadvantage on a rite.

#### Example Rite: Elemental Sentinel
*1st level rite, DC 18/15*
*Casting time: one day*
Primary check: Arcana
Secondary check (optional): Nature
Target: one object 
Duration: unlimited

You place a tiny elemental wisp within a single object, usually a mirror, statue, or other mundane-looking item, to serve as an alarm. Shattering or otherwise destroying the object frees the wisp and ends the ritual's effect even if the object is magically restored.  
  
**Critical Success** The elemental wisps inhabits the object and willingly serves you to the best of its ability. The wisp becomes aware of the target object's surroundings and can telepathically contact you with a range of 500 feet. It reports to you about intruders or other changes to the room in which it's located. You must tell the wisp what the criteria for the reports will be—for instance, when anyone not openly wearing a certain symbol enters the room or when an item is removed. The wisp has a +5 to its Perception and observes the room using normal vision, normal hearing, and touch.  
**Success** As critical success, but the range of the telepathy is 100 feet and the wisp's Perception modifier is +3.  
**Failure** You're unable to harness the spirit. The ritual fails.  
**Critical Failure** The elemental wisp is trapped in the object as with a success, but it refuses or is somehow unable to help you. The elemental might have extremely low Intelligence or simply be spiteful. Regardless, the object's reports are always misleading.
