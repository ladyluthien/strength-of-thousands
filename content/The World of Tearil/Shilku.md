Shilku, also known by its nickname of The Bloodcove, is a pirate city. Isolated from the rest of the peninsula by a coastal mountain range and defended from a sea assault by treacherous rocks at the mouth of the bay, crime and decadence have gone unchallenged here for generations. That being said, there are certain rules - and the so-called Knights of Abendego in [[Jula]] have been breaking them.
