The Cloud Peaks is a massive range of mountains that separates [[Amn]] from [[the Sword Coast]].  The tallest of their peaks can reach sixteen thousand feet above sea level, which makes them small in the grand scheme of Tearil's mountain ranges, but larger than any neighboring ranges. The only road that passes through these peaks is known simply as the Coast Road, guarded by two fortresses, Amnur and Rashturl. On the Amnian side, soft foothills have become a tree farm for the more industrial nation of Amn.
