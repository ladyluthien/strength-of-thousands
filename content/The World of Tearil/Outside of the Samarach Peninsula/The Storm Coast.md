The Storm Coast is the name for an unclaimed and mostly uncharted strip of land on the shores of **[[The Trackless Sea]]**. It is bordered to the south by the mercantile nation of **[[Amn]]**, although the **[[Cloud Peaks]]** that stretch almost from sea to sea prevent much passage except by a coastal route, and to the east by a great, trackless desert known as **[[The Anaurach]]**. To the north, mountains rise up to the **[[Spine of the World]]**.
