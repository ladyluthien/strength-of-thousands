Amn is a proud and mercantile nation south of the [[../The Storm Coast|The Storm Coast]] but north of the [[../../Samarach Peninsula|Samarach Peninsula]] that was ruled by dragons for much of its early history. Due to an alliance with the dwarves that dwell below its lands, it has grown profitable on the trade of gems, wood, and grain, which it produces in abundance. Contemporarily, it is ruled by a Council of Six designed to keep trade flowing smoothly. Each member of the council has a specific role; the disruption of monopolies, disagreements between merchants, taxes, trade routes, armies, and spies. The head of the Shadow Thieves, known only as the Silhouette, might as well be on the council for all the influence that they wield. 

Amn's draconic origins are visible throughout its culture. Dragonborn are not welcome there, despite their lack of culpability, and the oldest of Amn's buildings are built four to five times the size of their later counterparts to accommodate draconic bulk. It is said that the Amnian fondness for free trade and distaste of monopolies stems from the hoarding tendencies of the dragons.
