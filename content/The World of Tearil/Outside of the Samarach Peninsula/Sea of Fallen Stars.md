The Sea of Fallen Stars is an inland sea, trapped that way after some great cataclysm. However, a lucky confluence of springs, as well as a climate prone to rainfall, have kept it from drying out into a salt bed. It is bordered by several major powers, as well as having a cluster of semi-autonomous islands in the center. Legend holds that these islands are built from the bones of the first dragons, covered with coral. 

 The Sea of Fallen Stars is bordered by [[Amn]] to the east; [[the Anaurach]] to the north, albeit with Netheril's small vassal-state of [[Sembia]] in between the Anaurach proper and the sea; and [[Netheril]] to the west. To the south are a group of feuding city-states that change hands and names so rapidly that only those native to the region can keep track.
