Tearil is the main continent that our story takes place on. It is assumed by most to be flat, although the dwarves contend that this is not so and that they emerged, in fact, from a forge at the heart of it, equidistant from all areas of the world. Therefore the dwarves insist that Tearil is round, something generally dismissed as foolish; after all, then the people of [[Amn]] would fall right off.
