The Samarach Peninsula is the name given to the peninsula south of the Bay of Amn. Oral traditions place human settlement in the region going back almost twenty thousand years, making it one of the oldest confirmed human settlements in Tearil. The peninsula is a large (real-world comparison: if this region was on the west coast of Africa, it would span from Senegal to part of Nigeria) and densely forested region. Deep in the center of the jungle, it's said that the trees grow so tall and grand that black dragons can rest in their canopies. 

Communities who live in the jungle are deeply isolated from the rest of the world, and it would take a whole city of anthropologists to map them all (as well as at least another city's worth of adventurers to keep them safe from unfamiliar perils). The only commonly known one is the mysterious city of [[Mzali]], and even that one faces the ocean through a deeply secluded bay.  However, the coastal cities of [[Port Nantambu]], [[Shilku]] (also known as the Bloodcove), [[Senghar]], and [[Talashar]] are metropolitan hubs dealing in the trade of goods and ideas alike. [[Kibwe]] is a resource-rich mining city that employs laborers from all over, and the declining dwarven nation of [[Thindol]] rounds out the major population centers.
