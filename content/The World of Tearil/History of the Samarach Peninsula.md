Samarach was one of the first places where humans truly thrived following the Sundering. Separated from the other human nations by the Shining Sea to the north, it was spared much of the conflict of early Coramthan, Narfall, and Delemb. Following the Sundering, it experienced its fair share of conflict - droughts devastated the land, and the yuan-ti preyed upon the weak. Still, it also had its heroes - most notably Old-Mage Jatembe, the founder of the Magaambya, and his ten Magic Warriors. It is said that Jatembe managed to glimpse magic as it was before the Sundering, and built the Magaambya so that generations could work to restore such a beauty. 

Because of its relative remoteness, the eye of present-day [[Netheril]] has not yet turned to it. Such things are, for most residents, distant problems - maybe relevant someday, but not yet. [[Amn]]ian trade encroachment is a bigger worry, with some fearing that the cosmopolitan nation will use its economic power to steadily control more and more of the [[Samarach Peninsula]]. 

The exception is the Magaambya, which has been steadily growing as the only alternative to the magocracy of Netheril. For most of the other nations and peoples of Tearil, the Magaambya may be the only thing they know about the Samarach Peninsula. Some professors express concern about this, fearing that this diametric opposition not only stifles discovery, but drives undecided mages into the arms of Netheril, and advocate for intervention. However, most of the branches of the Magaambya train for discovery, not war, and the voices that say that the Magaambya must remain a beacon of peace and learning still hold dominant.
